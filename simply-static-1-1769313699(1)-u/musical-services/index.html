commercial & symphonic brass and woodwind section recordings: solo and section //

Sunrise Transparence provides professionally-recorded & mixed brass and woodwind solos & sections to a variety of artists and productions. Whether pop, rock, funk, dance, or an orchestral score, adding Sunrise Transparence horns to your tracks is a great way to add instinctive, human energy!

Available instruments:

Brass: trombone (tenor or bass), trumpet, euphonium, horn (concert or marching), tuba

Woodwinds: oboe, English horn, clarinet (soprano or bass), saxophone, flute, bassoon

Past projects range from 12-100+ layers, tailored to each artist’s vision. We provide separate raw close and room mic stems for each part for your mix engineer, or we can provide a cohesive stereo mixdown of your entire brass/woodwind section that’s ready to plug right into your track.

Arrangement and transcription services additionally available: send over your MIDI file, mockups, sketches, or score and hear it come to life…or kick back and let us do ALL the work! Send us your currently wind-less track, and we can write and/or improvise idiomatic parts for brass/woodwinds, custom-made to fill out your vision!

composition, arrangement, production services //

Sunrise Transparence offers the finest custom composition and arrangement services, in addition to providing production services ranging from recording live performers, to sample and synthesizer programming, as well as complete track production in a variety of genres.

We’re great with hybrid scores and textures that include both live instruments (see brass and woodwind services above) and electronic elements—whether you’re adding some synths for larger-than-life low end punch on your orchestral track, or adding the organic excitement of live brass to your dance track!
